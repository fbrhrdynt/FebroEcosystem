# VoltLab design direction

## Intent
A clear learning workspace for electrical students and technicians. Users should see where to continue, understand the next learning step, and place industrial devices deliberately on a panel.

## Liveliness dials
- Energy: 2 / 5 — measured and technical.
- Rhythm: 3 / 5 — change pace between the dashboard, learning sequence and hands-on canvas.
- Motion: 1 / 5 — keep controls direct; movement is reserved for circuit state and navigation.

## Decisions
- Use navy, steel gray, and restrained blue to match panel hardware and existing VoltLab identity. Use red/blue conductor colors only to distinguish electrical paths.
- Use realistic vector component drawings as technical illustrations; component names stay in standard engineering terminology in both languages.
- Start signed-in users at the dashboard; provide modules with objectives, prerequisites, lesson order, progress, and a clear next action.
- Keep DIN rail explicitly added from Mounting so the user controls panel arrangement and devices do not overlap on auto-generated rails.
- Mobile navigation has an explicit close button and backdrop. Provide visible focus indicators and paired language controls.
- Keep motion minimal and avoid fabricated social proof or dashboard data; counts come from the user's workspace.
- Give the public landing page a clear two-column demonstration and a four-column learning path that reflows to two columns on phones; the panel illustration must sit on a visible steel backplate, never a black fallback.
- Put Terms of Service and Privacy Policy on public URLs without requiring login, link them on the landing and sign-in screens, and provide Indonesian and English text for Google OAuth consent.
- Offer light and dark themes on the public and legal pages using the existing saved preference.
